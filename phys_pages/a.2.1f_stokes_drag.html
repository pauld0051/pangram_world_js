About this calculator

What it does

This calculator solves for drag force, dynamic viscosity, sphere radius, or relative speed from Stokes' law when the other three are known.

When it works

Use it for small spheres moving slowly through a fluid where the flow remains laminar and viscous forces dominate.

When it does not

Do not use this for high-speed drag, turbulent flow, or arbitrary shapes. Physics is rude like that.
