About this calculator

What it gives you

This calculates the magnitude of the upthrust produced by a displaced fluid, or works backwards to density, volume, or gravity.

When to use it

Use it whenever the buoyant force depends on the density of the fluid, the displaced volume, and the local gravitational field strength.

One thing to watch

Make sure the volume is the displaced volume, not automatically the full object volume unless the object is fully submerged. Physics loves that little trap.
